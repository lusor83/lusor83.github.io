BAGGAGE COLOR FLOW · LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Baggage Color Flow, a mobile game developed by DLF Technology. Baggage Color Flow is designed to work without creating a user account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number or postal address in order to play.

1. Who we are

DLF Technology is an independent software development business based in Italy. In this policy, “Baggage Color Flow”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the Baggage Color Flow mobile application and DLF Technology as its developer.

2. Information handled directly by Baggage Color Flow

Baggage Color Flow stores game progress and preferences locally on your device, such as unlocked levels, stars, best move counts, language, music and sound preferences. This local game data is used to provide gameplay and continuity between sessions.

Baggage Color Flow does not currently operate a proprietary remote account, cloud-save or analytics backend. Gameplay analytics events used by the application are kept locally/in memory for application operation and debugging and are not transmitted by DLF Technology to a proprietary analytics server.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Baggage Color Flow uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to provide advertising, including optional rewarded ads for one next-move hint, interstitial ads at eligible transitions after a win, and banner ads on the Home and level selection screens.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostic information and information used for advertising measurement, fraud prevention, frequency capping and, where permitted, ad personalization. The exact processing may depend on your device settings, region, consent choices and Google’s configuration.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Baggage Color Flow uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage consent before requesting advertising that requires such consent.

If Google indicates that privacy options must remain available, Baggage Color Flow provides access to the relevant privacy choices from within the application settings. You can also change certain advertising choices through your Android and Google account/device privacy settings.

5. Personalized and non-personalized advertising

Depending on applicable law, your consent status and Google’s eligibility rules, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized. Declining consent for personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no advertising will be shown; ads may instead be selected using limited or contextual signals as permitted by law and by Google’s policies.

6. No account registration

Baggage Color Flow does not currently require account registration or login. The app does not provide a feature for users to submit profile information, messages, photographs or other personal content to DLF Technology.

7. Data retention

You can remove local gameplay data using Android application storage controls or the game's progress-reset feature where available. Android system backup and restoration settings may also affect locally stored data. Information processed by Google or other advertising providers is retained according to their own policies and legal obligations.

8. Children and younger users

Baggage Color Flow is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. It does not ask players to provide their name, email address, telephone number or postal address to play.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to limit unnecessary collection of information and to keep gameplay data local where remote processing is not required. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may give you rights relating to personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent. For advertising consent managed through UMP, you may use the privacy options presented in Baggage Color Flow when those options are required and available.

11. Third-party services

Baggage Color Flow currently uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising and Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent management. The Android operating system, Google Play and distribution infrastructure may also process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Baggage Color Flow’s features, advertising configuration, third-party services or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL and will show its latest revision date.

13. Contact

For privacy or support requests relating to Baggage Color Flow, contact DLF Technology / LuSor Games at lusor83@gmail.com. Please identify the game in your message.

ITALIANO

Informativa sulla privacy

Ultimo aggiornamento: 29 ottobre 2026

La presente informativa descrive come vengono gestite le informazioni durante l’utilizzo di Baggage Color Flow, gioco mobile sviluppato da DLF Technology. Baggage Color Flow funziona senza richiedere la creazione di un account e non richiede nome, indirizzo e-mail, numero di telefono o indirizzo postale per poter giocare.

1. Dati gestiti direttamente dall’app

Baggage Color Flow salva localmente sul dispositivo i progressi e le preferenze di gioco, ad esempio livelli sbloccati, risultati, impostazioni e stato del tutorial. Questi dati servono esclusivamente al funzionamento del gioco e alla continuità tra le sessioni.

Attualmente Baggage Color Flow non utilizza un sistema proprietario remoto per account, salvataggi cloud o analytics. Gli eventi interni di gioco utilizzati dall’applicazione restano locali/in memoria per finalità operative e di debug e non vengono inviati da DLF Technology a un proprio server di analytics.

2. Pubblicità e Google AdMob

Baggage Color Flow utilizza Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads per mostrare pubblicità, inclusi annunci con premio facoltativi per un suggerimento sulla prossima mossa, interstitial durante le transizioni idonee dopo una vittoria e banner nella Home e nella selezione livelli.

Quando vengono richiesti o mostrati annunci, Google e i suoi partner pubblicitari possono trattare dati quali identificatori del dispositivo e pubblicitari, indirizzo IP, informazioni sul dispositivo e sull’app, interazioni con gli annunci, dati diagnostici e informazioni utilizzate per misurazione pubblicitaria, prevenzione delle frodi, limitazione della frequenza e, quando consentito, personalizzazione degli annunci. Il trattamento effettivo dipende anche da area geografica, impostazioni del dispositivo e scelte di consenso.

Per maggiori informazioni consulta Come Google utilizza i dati quando usi siti o app dei nostri partner e la Norme sulla privacy di Google.

3. Consenso e opzioni privacy

Dove richiesto dalla normativa, inclusi Spazio Economico Europeo, Regno Unito e Svizzera, Baggage Color Flow utilizza Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) per raccogliere o gestire il consenso prima di richiedere pubblicità che necessita di tale consenso.

Quando Google richiede che le opzioni privacy rimangano disponibili, Baggage Color Flow permette di accedervi dalle impostazioni dell’app.

4. Conservazione, diritti e contatti

I progressi locali restano sul dispositivo finché non vengono rimossi tramite i controlli dell’app, un eventuale reset o la disinstallazione. I dati trattati da Google o da altri fornitori pubblicitari sono conservati secondo le rispettive informative e gli obblighi di legge.

In base alla normativa applicabile puoi disporre di diritti quali accesso, rettifica, cancellazione, limitazione, opposizione o revoca del consenso. Per richieste relative a Baggage Color Flow scrivi a DLF Technology / LuSor Games: lusor83@gmail.com, indicando il nome del gioco.
