Your finances, easier to read.

My Finance Forecast is designed for everyday personal financial planning. It brings current-month tracking and future planning together in one clear mobile experience.

Income & expenses

Organize the movements that shape your monthly balance and keep the information you need in one place.

Financial forecast

Look ahead across upcoming months to understand the expected evolution of your personal finances.

Monthly recap

Review your month with a concise overview that helps you compare what happened with what you planned.

Clear mobile interface

Designed for quick daily use, with a focused interface built around personal finance rather than banking complexity.

Keep your financial information manageable with import and export capabilities designed around your own data.

Built for planning
