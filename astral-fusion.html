GAMEPLAY

Merge your way across the cosmos.

Astral Fusion combines a simple merge loop with physics, progression and collection. Every collision can create the next step in a 30-level cosmic evolution chain.

Physics merging

Celestial bodies move, collide and merge inside a dynamic arena governed by gravity and momentum.

30 evolution levels

Progress from Cosmic Dust through increasingly powerful astronomical forms toward the final evolution.

Score & combos

Chain successful merges, build combos and push for a higher score while keeping the arena under control.

Collection

Unlock and track the celestial forms you discover as your progression expands.

Achievements

Longer-term goals reward milestones, merge counts and progression beyond a single run.

Quick mobile sessions

A focused portrait-friendly loop makes it easy to play for a few minutes or chase a longer run.
