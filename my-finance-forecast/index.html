My Finance Forecast

Plan your cash flow before you spend. My Finance Forecast combines balances, recurring income and expenses, one-off events and debt instalments to show a deterministic month-by-month forecast.

Excel import/export

Import the official workbook locally and export a compatible Excel copy whenever you choose.

Google Drive is optional

Connect Drive only when you want to create a template or explicitly export/import supported files.

Privacy by default

Excel import is processed on the device and does not require Google Sign-In. The app does not automatically upload your financial plan. Google Drive is an optional feature and is used only after an explicit user action.

Google Drive access

When you choose to connect Google Drive, My Finance Forecast requests the per-file drive.file permission. This is used for files created or explicitly used with the app; the app does not request unrestricted access to your entire Drive.
