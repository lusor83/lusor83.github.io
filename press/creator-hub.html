CREATOR PLAYBOOK · NOVEMBER 2026

Three ready-to-film vertical video formats

These are filming briefs, not finished videos. Use original recordings from the released game and only show actions that occur in the current build. Keep overlays outside important controls and disclose sponsored relationships if any.

LOOKTWIST · 20 SECONDS

Find it before the reveal

0–3s: Show a genuine illustrated search scene. Overlay: “Can you spot it?”

3–12s: Hold the unaltered scene long enough for viewers to search; show a small countdown only if it does not obstruct clues.

12–17s: Zoom and reveal the actual target using in-game footage.

17–20s: “Try another scene in LookTwist” with the official Play link in the description.

Get LookTwist assets

ASTRAL FUSION · 18 SECONDS

One collision, one surprise

0–3s: Start on a real near-collision. Overlay: “What happens when these merge?”

3–13s: Capture the genuine physics interaction and resulting celestial merge in one continuous recording.

13–18s: Show the actual evolved object and invite viewers to try the next merge.

Do not fabricate a higher tier or imply a particular combination is possible without testing it.

Get Astral Fusion assets

BAGGAGE COLOR FLOW · 24 SECONDS

Which gate gets the suitcase?

0–4s: Start with a real level and an approaching suitcase. Overlay: “Can you route this bag?”

4–12s: Keep the actual conveyor layout visible so viewers can predict the correct switch direction.

12–20s: Show the real diverter change and the bag reaching its destination, without splicing together unrelated levels.

20–24s: Show the level result if captured and invite viewers to try Baggage Color Flow on Google Play.

Record genuine gameplay. If the attempted route fails, keep the authentic failure instead of inventing a successful outcome.

Get Baggage Color Flow assets

RUINSHOT · 22 SECONDS

Will this shot survive?

0–4s: Freeze briefly before an actual artillery turn. Overlay: “Would you take this shot?”

4–16s: Record the aim, projectile flight and impact in sequence, without substituting CGI for gameplay.

16–22s: Show the true outcome and the remaining fortress, then name RuinShot and link its official Google Play listing.

Use only real local multiplayer footage if describing a multiplayer match.

How to use these resources

  • Gameplay ideas are suggestions; capture and verify actual game footage before publishing a video. Do not present illustrative key art as gameplay.
  • Every press kit links to official images and source descriptions. Check screenshot captions and language before uploading; many source captures are in Italian.
  • You are free to form an independent opinion. Editorial consideration and honest coverage are not conditional on positive reviews or payments.
  • Google Play links point to the official developer listings. Availability can vary by region and device. Some referral links carry campaign tags for aggregate attribution in Play Console; they do not guarantee install measurement.
  • For an interview, high-resolution art, or permission questions, email lusor83@gmail.com.
