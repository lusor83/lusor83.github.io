Short description

RuinShot is a mobile 2D turn-based artillery and fortress-building game. Players design a modular defense, protect their Command Core, exchange ballistic fire and reposition surviving parts or destroyed wreckage between shots.

Key features

  • Modular fortress construction and saved designs
  • Physics-based ballistic aiming and destruction
  • Tactical Action Points between attacks
  • Persistent wreckage that can become improvised cover
  • 20 single-player campaign missions across five arenas
  • Five ballistic weapons with campaign unlocks
  • Destructible central obstacles and modular fortress parts
  • Standalone CPU battles with three difficulty choices
  • Interactive tutorial, original music and sound effects
  • Local two-player pass-and-play
  • Command Core and weapon-elimination victory conditions

Release status

The current build includes 20 solo missions, five weapons, five arenas, the Fortress Lab, local two-player pass-and-play, tactical Action Points, persistent wreckage, destructible obstacles, an interactive tutorial, music and sound effects. Production build 14 (0.3.0-rc.5) is active in 178 countries/regions. The public listing and Install button were verified without an account on 2 November 2026. Download on Google Play.

Audience: artillery, physics and strategy players who enjoy designing defenses and adapting after each turn.
