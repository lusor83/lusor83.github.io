Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This policy describes how DLF Technology handles information in connection with this website and its mobile applications. App-specific disclosures shown on Google Play remain an integral part of the information provided to users.

1. Who we are

DLF Technology is an independent software development business based in Italy, focused on mobile games and digital products.

2. Information collected through this website

This website is a static informational website hosted through GitHub Pages. DLF Technology does not use account registration, contact forms or proprietary tracking scripts on this website.

The hosting provider may process technical information necessary to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser information, request logs and security-related data, according to its own terms and privacy practices.

3. Information collected through our mobile applications

Our applications may process information only when required for their features, operation, security, distribution or monetization. The exact categories of data used by each application are disclosed in the relevant Google Play listing and, where appropriate, in additional app-specific notices.

DLF Technology does not intentionally request personal information that is unnecessary for the operation of its applications.

4. Third-party services

Our applications may rely on platform or third-party services such as Google Play services, crash reporting, analytics, advertising or other infrastructure. When such services are used, they may process technical or usage information under their own privacy policies. The relevant disclosures will be reflected in the application's Google Play Data safety information.

5. Children

Where an application is intended for or may be used by children, DLF Technology will apply the additional requirements applicable to that application and its target audience, including Google Play policies and applicable data-protection rules.

6. Data retention and security

Where DLF Technology directly processes personal information, it is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing. Requests can be submitted through the developer contact information made available on the relevant Google Play listing.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our applications, services or legal obligations change. The current version will always be published on this page with its latest revision date.

9. Contact

For privacy or support requests, please use the developer contact information published on the relevant Google Play application page.
