Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy describes how DLF Technology handles information in connection with this website and its mobile applications, including My Finance Forecast, LookTwist, Astral Fusion, Life Unwritten and Nerelune: Secrets of the Cove. App-specific disclosures shown on Google Play remain an integral part of the information provided to users.

1. Who we are

DLF Technology is an independent software development business based in Italy, focused on Android apps, games and digital products.

2. Information used through this website

This website is a static informational website hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not provide account registration or contact forms and it does not currently use third-party audience analytics such as Google Analytics.

To understand where promotional visits originate, the website may read standard campaign parameters included in the page URL, such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content and utm_term. These campaign labels may be stored in the browser's local storage and used to preserve campaign context when a visitor follows a Google Play link.

Campaign labels do not by themselves contain information intended to directly identify a person. When a visitor chooses a Google Play link, relevant campaign labels may be included in the Play Store referrer parameter and are therefore transmitted to Google as part of that navigation.

The hosting provider may process technical information necessary to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser information, request logs and security-related data, according to its own terms and privacy practices.

3. Google Play links and external resources

Pages for applications available in production may contain links and official badge assets provided by Google Play. Loading those resources or following those links may cause the visitor's browser to connect to Google services, which may receive standard technical request information under Google's own privacy terms.

4. Information collected through our mobile applications

Our applications may process information only when required for their features, operation, security, distribution or monetization. The exact categories of data used by each application are disclosed in the relevant Google Play listing and, where appropriate, in additional app-specific notices.

DLF Technology does not intentionally request personal information that is unnecessary for the operation of its applications.

5. Third-party services

Our applications may rely on platform or third-party services such as Google Play services, cloud storage integrations, crash reporting, analytics, advertising or other infrastructure. When such services are used, they may process technical or usage information under their own privacy policies. Relevant disclosures are reflected in the application's Google Play Data safety information.

6. Children

Where an application is intended for or may be used by children, DLF Technology will apply the additional requirements applicable to that application and its target audience, including Google Play policies and applicable data-protection rules.

7. Data retention and security

Campaign information stored by the website through browser local storage remains on the user's device unless the browser data is cleared or overwritten by a later campaign visit. Where DLF Technology directly processes personal information, it is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing. Requests can be submitted through the developer contact information made available on the relevant Google Play listing.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our applications, website features, analytics configuration, services or legal obligations change. The current version will always be published on this page with its latest revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy or support requests, please use the developer contact information published on the relevant Google Play application page.
