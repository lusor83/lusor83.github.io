FUTURE BALANCE PLANNING

Future Balance Planners on Android

Updated 5 October 2026

A future balance planner starts with what you have today and projects how recurring and one-off movements may change your available balance over time.

Start from the current balance

The forecast becomes useful when it connects today's available money with expected future movements.

Recurring items create a baseline

Salary, rent, loans and subscriptions form the predictable core of the projection.

One-off costs make the plan realistic

Travel, insurance, repairs and other irregular expenses can be added before they happen instead of being discovered afterward.
