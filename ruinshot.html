CORE LOOP

Build. Protect. Aim. Fire. Destroy. Adapt.

RuinShot combines fortress construction with turn-based artillery and a battlefield that keeps changing after every shot.

Fortress Lab

Build from scratch, edit a saved design or start from a preset while balancing structural protection, weapons and the mandatory Command Core.

Physics artillery

Select an operational launcher, adjust angle and power, then fire a ballistic projectile into a destructible enemy fortress.

Tactical repositioning

Spend limited Action Points before attacking to move surviving fortress parts or wreckage and reshape your defense.

Persistent wreckage

Destroyed objects remain on the battlefield. Reposition debris to create improvised cover and protect critical structures.

Two ways to win

Destroy the opponent's Command Core, or leave them with no operational offensive weapon when their next turn begins.

Solo & local multiplayer

The current playable slice includes single-player battles against CPU opponents and local two-player pass-and-play on the same device.
