IN DEVELOPMENT

No two lives need to follow the same path.

Life Unwritten is a persistent portrait life simulator where time advances, choices accumulate and multiple systems interact to create a personal story.

Persistent life

Grow from childhood through education, work and adulthood while your decisions remain part of the same evolving life.

Study & careers

Choose schools, university paths, professions and alternative careers that open different opportunities.

Relationships

Build persistent connections with family and other characters, with events and narrative arcs shaped over time.

Health & energy

Balance stress, fatigue, fitness, illness and recovery alongside everything else competing for your time.

Money & ambition

Manage salaries, savings, contracts, investments, assets, debt and the financial consequences of your choices.

Skill challenges

Important moments can use deterministic TriPeaks challenges to add a skill-based layer to the simulation.
