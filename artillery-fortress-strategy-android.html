ANDROID STRATEGY · GAMEPLAY GUIDE

Artillery fortress strategy: aim, build and adapt

In a turn-based artillery game, the interesting decision is rarely just where to shoot. It's what your shot leaves behind — and whether your fortress can survive the reply. RuinShot combines ballistic aiming, modular fortresses and destructible battlefields on Android.

1. Aim with an objective, not just a target

Before firing, decide what you want the shot to accomplish. A direct hit can damage an exposed part of a fortress, while a shot that changes the surrounding structure may alter the next turn. Angle and power are part of the tactical choice, not merely controls to master.

2. Build for the return shot

Modular fortress construction changes how you approach defense. Consider which components are exposed, which are protected and how a damaged section might affect the rest of your position. A visually impressive build is not necessarily the most resilient one.

3. Reassess after each impact

RuinShot's persistent wreckage means the battlefield does not simply reset between turns. After every impact, look again: an opening may have appeared, a previously safe area may be vulnerable, or an indirect route may become possible. Treat each turn as a new tactical puzzle.

4. Practice with different weapon choices

RuinShot's official game description identifies five weapons and 20 solo missions. Rather than assuming one weapon solves every encounter, experiment with different trajectories and learn how a given shot interacts with a specific structure. The game also includes local two-player battles.

5. Make your gameplay footage tell the story

For a short video, capture a real aiming turn, the impact and the altered battlefield. That before-and-after sequence explains the game more clearly than an unrelated cinematic image. Use authentic game capture and label promotional artwork as artwork.
