Ruinshot ? Privacy Policy

Developer: DLF Technology / LuSor Games, Italy
Support and privacy contact: lusor83@gmail.com

Ruinshot can be played offline and does not require an account. The Android advertising edition includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which use an Internet connection. No in-app purchases, cloud saves or separate developer analytics service are included.

ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY DATA
Ads may appear on the main menu and level selection, and between eligible completed matches. A video ad for one aiming suggestion is optional. You can keep playing when ads are unavailable or without using rewarded help.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share an IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. The developer does not upload your fortress designs or saved campaign to AdMob.

See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

PRIVACY CHOICES
The game refreshes UMP consent information at launch and requests ads only after the returned status permits them. Where required and available, Google's consent form lets you manage advertising choices. Settings includes AD PRIVACY OPTIONS when UMP requires this entry point. If consent cannot be resolved, ads stay unavailable and the game remains playable. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google handles SDK data under its own policy and controls; contact us for questions about the game's own local data or support correspondence.

Your fortress designs, campaign progress, tutorial completion, preferences and any local battle checkpoint are stored in the app's private storage on this device. A previous valid save may be retained locally to recover damaged files. These files are not uploaded by Ruinshot.

Android or the store may provide their own backup, distribution and diagnostic services under their respective settings and privacy policies. These platform services are separate from data transmission by the game.

Removing the app's data or uninstalling can remove locally stored progress. Keep a verified copy before changing installations or devices. Ruinshot does not currently offer a cloud account or cross-device synchronization.

If you contact lusor83@gmail.com, any information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.

This notice describes the advertising edition. Earlier builds up to dev.3/rc.3 did not include an advertising SDK. Material changes to services will be reflected in this notice and the store declarations.
