## My Finance Forecast

### Product Hunt
**Name:** My Finance Forecast
**Tagline:** See your future balance before the month arrives
**Short description:** An Android personal-finance app focused on forward-looking cash-flow planning. Start from today's balance, add recurring and one-off movements, and see how your finances may evolve over the coming months.
**Maker comment:** Most finance apps tell you where your money went. I built My Finance Forecast around a different question: what is likely to happen next? **Google Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lusor83.myfinanceforecast
